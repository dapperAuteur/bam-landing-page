## Life at a Glance

**World Record Holder.** Shattered the 100m world record for the M100 age group with a time of 26.34 seconds.

**Pioneering Entrepreneur.** Opened the first Black-owned dental lab in Monmouth County, NJ.

**80-Year Marriage.** Shared a lifetime of love and partnership with his wife, Adele.

**WWII Veteran.** Served with distinction in the Battle of the Bulge, earning 4 Bronze Battle Stars.

**The Wright Philosophy.** "It all starts with self-belief... I think it`s in the head more than it is physical."

**Decades of Dominance.** Broke the M75 200m world record at age 76 and won the Penn Relays 100m at 77.

## A Century's Journey

1. **1922.** Born in Long Branch, New Jersey.
2. **1930s.** Becomes a high school track star and meets his future wife, Adele.
3. **WWII.** Serves in the US Army, fighting in the Battle of the Bulge.
4. **Post-WWII.** Opens the first Black-owned dental lab in Monmouth County.
5. **1998.** At 76, breaks the M75 200-meter world record.
6. **1999.** At 77, wins the 75-and-over 100m dash at the Penn Relays.
7. **2022.** Celebrates his 100th birthday and sets a new 100m world record (M100) the next day.
