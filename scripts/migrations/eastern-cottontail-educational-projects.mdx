## Why Teachers Love These Projects

**Standards Aligned.** Every project directly addresses Indiana Science Standards

**Cross-Curricular.** Integrates Math, ELA, Art, and Social Studies naturally

**Local Connection.** Features wildlife students can observe in their community

## Kindergarten Projects

Indiana Standard: K-LS1-1 - Animals need food, water, and shelter

### Cottontail Needs Chart

Students create visual charts comparing cottontail and human basic needs

**Duration.** 45 minutes

**Featured Episode:** Episode 1: Suburban Survivors

**Materials Needed.**

- Construction paper
- Magazines for cutting
- Glue sticks
- Markers

**Assessment.** Students explain one thing cottontails need to survive

**Cross-Curricular Extensions.**

- **Math:** Counting needs (1-5)
- **ELA:** Vocabulary building: shelter, habitat, survival
- **Art:** Drawing and collaging

### Build a Backyard Habitat

Design shoebox habitats showing food, water, and shelter needs

**Duration.** 60 minutes

**Featured Episode:** Episode 1: Suburban Survivors

**Materials Needed.**

- Shoeboxes
- Natural materials
- Craft supplies
- Animal figures

**Assessment.** Portfolio with labeled habitat components

**Cross-Curricular Extensions.**

- **Math:** Measuring spaces, counting objects
- **ELA:** Descriptive words for habitat features
- **Art:** 3D construction and design

## Grade 1 Projects

Indiana Standard: 1-LS1-2 - Animal behaviors help survival; 1-LS3-1 - Young animals are like parents

### Cottontail Behavior Book

Create illustrated books showing survival behaviors

**Duration.** 90 minutes (2 sessions)

**Featured Episode:** Episode 2: The Killing Fields

**Materials Needed.**

- Paper
- Stapler
- Colored pencils
- Behavior cards

**Assessment.** Book demonstrates understanding of 3+ behaviors

**Cross-Curricular Extensions.**

- **Math:** Sequencing pages (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- **ELA:** Simple sentence writing, illustration matching
- **Art:** Book illustration and design

### Baby Animals Investigation

Compare baby and adult cottontails through observation

**Duration.** 45 minutes

**Featured Episode:** Episode 3: The Matriarch's Gamble

**Materials Needed.**

- Comparison charts
- Magnifying glasses
- Animal photos

**Assessment.** Identify 2 similarities and 1 difference

**Cross-Curricular Extensions.**

- **Math:** Size comparisons, measurement
- **ELA:** Comparison vocabulary
- **Art:** Scientific drawing
